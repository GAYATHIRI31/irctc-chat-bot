IRCTC CUSTOMER SERVICE KNOWLEDGE BASE
======================================

1. TICKET BOOKING
------------------
- Tickets can be booked online at www.irctc.co.in or via the IRCTC Rail Connect mobile app.
- Users must register on IRCTC portal before booking tickets.
- Booking opens 60 days in advance (excluding the date of journey).
- Tatkal booking opens 1 day in advance (excluding date of journey).
  - AC classes: Tatkal booking opens at 10:00 AM
  - Non-AC classes: Tatkal booking opens at 11:00 AM
- Maximum 6 passengers can be booked per transaction.
- A user can book maximum 12 tickets per month (6 extra with Aadhaar-verified account).

2. PAYMENT METHODS
-------------------
- Credit Card / Debit Card (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay)
- Net Banking
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
- IRCTC iPay Wallet
- EMI options available on select cards
- No convenience fee on UPI payments up to ₹2000.
- Convenience fee varies by payment method and class of travel.

3. TICKET TYPES
----------------
- E-Ticket: Booked online, can be shown on mobile (no printout needed).
- I-Ticket: Delivered by post (booking must be done 4-5 days in advance).
- Counter Ticket: Booked at railway station counters.
- Platform Ticket: For entry to platform without travel (costs ₹10).

4. PNR STATUS
--------------
- PNR (Passenger Name Record) is a 10-digit number on your ticket.
- Check PNR status at: www.irctc.co.in or SMS "PNR <number>" to 139.
- Status types:
  - CNF: Confirmed berth allotted
  - WL: Waitlisted
  - RAC: Reservation Against Cancellation (share berth)
  - PQWL: Pooled Quota Waitlist
  - GNWL: General Waitlist
  - RLWL: Remote Location Waitlist

5. CANCELLATION POLICY
------------------------
- E-tickets can be cancelled online via IRCTC website or app.
- Counter tickets must be cancelled at railway counters.
- Cancellation charges:
  - AC First Class / Executive Class: ₹240 flat charge
  - AC 2 Tier / First Class: ₹200 flat charge
  - AC 3 Tier / AC Chair Car / AC 3 Economy: ₹180 flat charge
  - Sleeper Class: ₹120 flat charge
  - Second Class: ₹60 flat charge
- No refund if cancelled less than 4 hours before departure.
- Tatkal tickets: No refund on cancellation (except in special cases).
- Waitlisted tickets cancelled automatically if not confirmed — full refund given.

6. REFUND POLICY
-----------------
- Refunds are processed within 3-7 working days for online payments.
- Refund is credited back to the original payment source.
- TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) can be filed for special refund cases:
  - Train cancelled by Railways
  - Train running more than 3 hours late
  - Passenger not travelled due to Railways fault
- TDR must be filed within 72 hours of scheduled departure.

7. TRAIN CLASSES
-----------------
- 1A – First AC: Most premium, 4 berths per coupe, full privacy
- 2A – Second AC: 2-tier sleeper, curtains provided
- 3A – Third AC: 3-tier sleeper, most popular
- 3E – Third AC Economy: Budget 3-tier AC
- FC – First Class: Non-AC, comfortable, lockable coupe
- SL – Sleeper Class: Most common, non-AC, 8 berths per bay
- 2S – Second Sitting: Non-AC seat, short journeys
- CC – AC Chair Car: Reclining seats, used in Shatabdi/Vande Bharat
- EC – Executive Chair Car: Premium seats in Shatabdi/Vande Bharat

8. IRCTC ACCOUNT
-----------------
- Register at www.irctc.co.in with a valid email and mobile number.
- OTP verification required during registration.
- Aadhaar linking increases monthly ticket booking limit.
- Password must be 8-15 characters with letters, numbers and special characters.
- Account gets locked after 6 consecutive failed login attempts.
- To unlock account: use "Forgot Password" or contact IRCTC helpdesk.

9. SENIOR CITIZEN & CONCESSION
--------------------------------
- Senior Citizens (Men 60+, Women 58+) get concession on select trains.
- Concession: 40% for men, 50% for women in most classes.
- Concession currently suspended on most trains (check IRCTC for updates).
- Other concessions available for: Students, Disabled persons, Cancer patients, Journalists, etc.
- Concession must be selected at time of booking and ID proof must be shown during journey.

10. TRAIN TYPES
----------------
- Rajdhani Express: Premium train connecting state capitals to New Delhi, fully AC.
- Shatabdi Express: Day intercity train, fully AC, fast.
- Duronto Express: Non-stop long distance train.
- Vande Bharat Express: Modern semi-high speed, self-propelled, fully AC.
- Garib Rath: Budget AC train with lower fares.
- Superfast Express: Runs at higher speed, extra superfast surcharge applies.
- Mail/Express: Regular trains connecting major cities.
- Passenger/Ordinary: Slow, stops at every station, cheapest.
- Jan Shatabdi: Cheaper version of Shatabdi, partially non-AC.

11. LUGGAGE POLICY
-------------------
- Free luggage allowance per passenger:
  - AC First Class: 70 kg
  - AC 2 Tier / First Class: 50 kg
  - AC 3 Tier / AC Chair Car: 40 kg
  - Sleeper / Second Class: 40 kg
- Excess luggage must be booked at the luggage office at the station.
- Prohibited items: Flammable materials, explosives, acid, weapons.

12. FOOD & CATERING
--------------------
- IRCTC provides e-catering service on selected trains.
- Order food via: www.ecatering.irctc.co.in or call 1323.
- Food can be ordered from restaurants at upcoming stations during the journey.
- Pantry car available in most long-distance trains.
- Rajdhani and Shatabdi trains include meals in ticket price.

13. DIVYAANG (DIFFERENTLY ABLED) FACILITIES
---------------------------------------------
- Reserved quota for Divyaang passengers in all trains.
- 75% concession for certain disability categories.
- Wheelchair assistance available at major stations — request in advance.
- Accessible coaches and facilities at major railway stations.

14. RAILWAY PASS / SEASON TICKET
----------------------------------
- Monthly Season Tickets (MST) available for daily commuters.
- Quarterly Season Tickets (QST) also available.
- Available for Second Class and Sleeper Class on specific routes.
- Must be purchased at railway counter with valid ID proof.

15. LOST / MISPLACED TICKET
-----------------------------
- E-ticket: No issue — re-download from IRCTC website or app and show on mobile.
- Counter ticket lost: File an indemnity bond at the nearest railway station.
- A duplicate ticket may be issued at the station on payment of a fee.

16. COMPLAINT & GRIEVANCE
---------------------------
- Rail Madad App: Official complaint and grievance app by Indian Railways.
- Helpline Numbers:
  - IRCTC Customer Care: 14646 / 0755-6610661 / 0755-4090600
  - Indian Railways Helpline: 139 (24x7, multilingual)
  - Railway Security (RPF): 182
  - Food Complaint Helpline: 1800-111-321 (toll free)
- Online complaint: www.railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in
- Twitter: @IRCTCofficial / @RailMinIndia

17. STATION & TRAIN ENQUIRY
-----------------------------
- Train running status: SMS "TRAIN <number>" to 139 or check IRCTC app.
- Arrival/departure info: Call 139 or check National Train Enquiry System (NTES).
- Station code lookup: Available on IRCTC website and app.
- Coach position enquiry: Available at stations and on IRCTC app.

18. IRCTC TOURISM PACKAGES
----------------------------
- IRCTC offers tour packages including Bharat Darshan, Buddhist Circuit, etc.
- Packages include train, hotel, sightseeing and meals.
- Book at: www.irctctourism.com
- Special pilgrim trains for Char Dham, Tirupati, Shirdi, etc.

19. WAITLIST RULES
-------------------
- Waitlisted passengers are NOT allowed to board if WL is not confirmed.
- Exception: RAC passengers are allowed to travel and share a berth.
- Final chart is prepared 4 hours before departure.
- Check final chart status on IRCTC app or website.

20. CHILD TICKET POLICY
-------------------------
- Children below 5 years: Free travel (no berth provided).
- Children 5-11 years: Half fare with berth, full fare without berth.
- Children 12 years and above: Full adult fare.
